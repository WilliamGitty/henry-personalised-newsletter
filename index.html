Poundland collapses putting 11,000 jobs at risk

Poundland has collapsed into administration, putting 11,000 jobs at risk, after its owners failed to find a buyer to keep it in business. The discount retailer formally entered administration on Wednesday afternoon, just weeks before Christmas. Poundland's US owners held talks with several bidders over a last-minute rescue deal for the business, but no transaction was reached. This led to Poundland's boss filing for administration, with consultancy Alix Partners appointed to oversee the process.
