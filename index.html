Lindsay Clancy to face retrial next year for deaths of her three children

Lindsay Clancy, the Massachusetts woman accused of murdering her three children, will face a second trial next year, a judge has said. The judge set a trial date for 29 March 2027, though the district attorney did not explicitly say what charges Clancy would face. Prosecutors said they wanted to proceed with a jury trial, and the defence said they took that to mean Clancy would again face first-degree murder charges, the same as she faced in the first trial.

Ed Miliband said he will "definitely not" resign if the government approves the Rosebank oil field, despite previously calling the scheme "climate vandalism".
