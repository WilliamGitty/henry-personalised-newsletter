Kenyan doomsday cult leader convicted over further 123 deaths

A court in Kenya has convicted the leader of a doomsday Christian cult of the manslaughter of 123 of his followers. Paul Mackenzie was found guilty in Mombasa of ordering his followers, all adults, to fast to death in Shakahola forest near the coast between January 2021 and September 2023. Earlier this month Mackenzie was jailed for 200 years for murdering 191 people, all but one of them children, in the same mass killing.
