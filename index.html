US loses measles elimination status after 26 years amid record case numbers

After 26 years, the US has lost its status of having eliminated the highly contagious measles virus, following two years of record case numbers and deaths. The Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), a specialised service of the United Nations, determined the US has had uninterrupted transmission of the same strain of measles for more than 12 months. States have reported 10 measles-related deaths this year, the most the country has seen since the 1990s.
