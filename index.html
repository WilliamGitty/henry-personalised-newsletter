Severe drought declared in parts of southern England

A severe drought has been declared across parts of southern England after months of low rainfall. Parts of Cornwall, Devon, Dorset, Somerset, East Sussex and West Sussex are affected. Supplies have been significantly impacted by high demand following the hottest summer on record.

Why Starlink internet is so important on the battlefield in Ukraine

Starlink satellites operated by Elon Musk's SpaceX provide high-speed internet - something modern war relies heavily on. Crew in Lewis fishing boat tragedy named by police

A fisherman whose body was found after a boat went missing off the Western Isles on Tuesday has been named as Douglas Craigie. The 49-year-old's crew-mates, Norman Murray, who is 40 and known as Tam, and 23-year-old Murdo Morrison are still missing. The three men, who are all from Lewis, had been fishing in a boat called Kilda. The vessel disappeared during stormy weather off Port of Ness in north Lewis. The search for the missing crew and boat has resumed after being suspended overnight.
