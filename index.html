Boat carrying some 270 people capsizes off northern Cyprus - reports

A passenger boat with some 270 people on board has capsized off northern Cyprus, with a search and rescue under way, local media report. Manhunt after shooting at Swiss rave kills woman, 22, and injures five

A manhunt is under way after a 22-year-old woman was killed and five others injured in a shooting at a rave in Switzerland. The woman died at the scene in Aarau, police said.
