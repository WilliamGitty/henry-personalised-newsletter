Man, 80, who died in light aircraft crash near Loch Lomond named

An 80-year-old man who died in a light aircraft crash is understood to be flying instructor Ian Haggis. He and a 16-year-old boy died when their plane came down near Loch Lomond. The teenager's identity has not been released. The pair had flown out from Glasgow Airport at 16:17 and stopped transmitting at 16:37, a few miles north east of the village of Drymen near Stirling. Emergency services were called to the scene of the crash at about 16:45 on Friday.

Polanski says he will not overturn Green Party's 'Zionism is racism' motion

Zack Polanski has said he will not overturn a motion passed by Green Party members that declared Zionism a form of racism. The Green leader told Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg that, while he would like to make amendments, it would be party policy until 2028. The motion, passed at the party's conference earlier this month, described Zionism as an ideology supporting "an ethnonationalist Jewish state in historic Palestine to the exclusion and/or domination of the non-Jewish population".

Pope Leo XIV thanks wellwishers in first event after cancer surgery

Pope Leo XIV held his first public engagement since his lung cancer surgery on Sunday, as he recited the Angelus prayer from the window of Vatican's Apostolic Palace. The surgery had taken place just a few days before, after Pope Leo had been diagnosed with lung adenocarcinoma, the most common form of lung cancer. After the prayer, he thanked the people who have prayed for him in recent days and said he appreciated the support.

Police officer in hospital after being shot in Nottingham

A police officer is being treated in hospital after being shot in Nottingham city centre. Nottinghamshire Police said the officer had approached two suspects in Angel Row shortly before 04:50 BST on Sunday. The officer is now being treated for a wound to his foot, the force added. Detectives said a firearm was recovered and that two suspects, a man and a woman, fled the scene. Angel Row is currently closed and a large police scene remains in place.
