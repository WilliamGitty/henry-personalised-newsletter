English pupils ahead of rest of UK in international school tests

Pupils in England have outperformed those in Scotland, Northern Ireland and Wales in international tests sat by hundreds of thousands of teenagers around the world. England ranked above the other home nations in the Programme for International Student Assessment (Pisa) across all of reading, science and maths. However the latest Pisa report, which is published every three years, also warned of a worldwide decline in teenagers' reading skills.
