Wildlife park remains closed after fatal tiger attack

Yorkshire Wildlife Park remains closed after a man climbed into a tiger enclosure and was fatally attacked by the animal. Emergency services were called to the park near Doncaster, South Yorkshire, at about 13:30 BST on Saturday after reports of a man climbing down into the enclosure from a walkway. An eyewitness described seeing the man wearing headphones and "pacing around" near the enclosure before he went in and was attacked. Police said the man sadly died at the scene.

Children among 109 dead after boat capsizes off Djibouti, UN says

At least 109 migrants including 15 children have died and dozens more are still missing after a boat sank off the coast of Djibouti, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) has said. The boat was travelling from Djibouti to Yemen with 350 people on board, when it capsized near the port town of Obock on Thursday night. Around 80 people were rescued, the IOM said.
