Welsh Grand National-winning jockey O'Brien dies aged 39

Jockey Tom O'Brien has died at the age of 39 after being diagnosed with cancer. Irishman O'Brien rode more than 1,000 winners in Britain and Ireland, including two at the Cheltenham Festival and the Welsh Grand National twice. The nephew of trainer Aidan O'Brien, he was a mainstay of the Minehead operation run by Philip Hobbs, joining as a teenager and remaining until his retirement in 2023.

Four grizzly bears killed in Canada after fatal attack on mother and daughter

Four grizzly bears have been killed in connection with a fatal attack on a mother and daughter in the Canadian province of British Columbia. Jennifer Balcarras, 48, and her daughter Selah Reed, 20, died earlier this month after going on an afternoon walk in a trail area in Bella Coola. The British Columbia Conservation Office Service (BCCOS) launched an investigation into their deaths and forensic testing has since confirmed a sow and her three cubs were responsible for the attack.
