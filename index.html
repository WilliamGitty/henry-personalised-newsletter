Mother of Ghanaian boy killed in US 'wants justice' as she flies for funeral

The mother of a 15-year-old Ghanaian boy who was fatally shot in the United States last week has left Ghana for America after being granted an emergency visa by President Donald Trump. "I want justice for my son," Doris Adu Gyamfi told the BBC at the airport before her departure. She said he had dreamt of becoming a medical doctor.
