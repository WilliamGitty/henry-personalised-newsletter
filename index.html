Five teenagers stabbed at Halloween party

Five teenagers have been seriously injured after being stabbed at a Halloween party, police have said. Officers were called to Park Road in Chandler's Ford, near Eastleigh, shortly after 10:30 GMT on Saturday. The injured teens - three boys aged 16 and two aged 17 - were taken to hospital. Hampshire Police said it believes an unknown group gained entry to a party at an address on Park Road before the stabbings. The force has appealed for witnesses and anyone with information to come forward.
