Timetable set out for dismantling leasehold system in England and Wales

The government has set out a timetable for when the leasehold system in England and Wales will be dismantled. Under the plans, most new flats will be sold as commonhold, where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. The government says a modernised commonhold model is expected to be up and running around 2029, followed by a ban on the sale of most new leasehold flats, expected around 2031.
