What is the EU-UK youth experience scheme and when will it start?

The UK and EU have agreed a youth experience scheme which will allow a limited number of 18 to 31-year-olds to work, study or travel in the European Union for up to three years. Every year, 100,000 Britons will be able to participate in the scheme, with a reciprocal arrangement for 100,000 EU citizens. A joint EU-UK statement says the scheme is due to launch no later than January 2030.

Daniel Thomas jailed for 15 months for slashing dinghy

Anti-migrant activist Daniel Thomas has been jailed for 15 months for slashing a dinghy with a knife in the English Channel. The 37-year-old, known to his supporters as Danny Tommo, live-streamed himself cutting the boat while a French rescue worker was on board in September. Sentencing him, a judge called Thomas a "publicity seeker" whose actions were a "flagrant interference" with the work of rescue crews.

Nearly 600 women killed in South Africa within three months

Nearly 600 women were killed in South Africa between July and September this year, according to the country's latest crime statistics. This figure includes 13 women whose partially naked and bruised bodies were found dumped in public areas in and around Johannesburg. The murders triggered national outrage and police faced pressure to find the culprits, in a country with one of the highest rates of gender-based violence and murder rates in the world.
