A childminder who admitted leaving children she was caring for alone and unsupervised in her home has been jailed for two years. Tube drivers to stage strikes in Christmas run-up

London Underground drivers are to stage a series of 24-hour strikes in a long-running dispute over working arrangements, the Rail, Maritime and Transport (RMT) union has announced. A series of walkouts will begin on 26 November, with further strikes planned for 17 December, 19 December, 4 January, 6 January, 17 February, 23 February and 25 February.
