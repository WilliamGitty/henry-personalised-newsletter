A simple guide to the midterms

The US midterm elections take place on Tuesday, when voters take part in a ballot that could have a major impact on the rest of Donald Trump's presidency. Americans will choose members of Congress, which is made up of two parts - the House of Representatives and the Senate. Elections take place every two years, and when they happen in the middle of the president's four-year term of office, they are called the midterms.
