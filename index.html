King Charles, Macron and Burnham to preview Bayeux Tapestry display

King Charles, French President Emmanuel Macron and UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham are to preview of one of the blockbuster cultural events of the year, when they get to view the Bayeux Tapestry at the British Museum. The visit on Wednesday comes ahead of the medieval tapestry going on public display in London from 10 September. The 70m (230ft) tapestry, originally embroidered in England but kept for centuries in France, tells the story of the Norman Conquest and the Battle of Hastings in 1066.
